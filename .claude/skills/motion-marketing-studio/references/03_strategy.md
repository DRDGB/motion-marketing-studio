# Phase 3: Strategy and content calendar

Output: `03_STRATEGY.md` + `CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv` (30 or 60 days) + first entries in
`PRODUCTION_QUEUE.csv`. Everything is PROPOSED until the user approves it.

## 1. Positioning (one paragraph, then one line)
"For <audience> who <need>, <brand> is the <category> that <key benefit>, because <proof>."
Then the **channel promise**: what a viewer gets from following (e.g. "60-second answers to the questions
customers ask before they buy", "the kitchen secrets behind your favourite dish", "how to choose a home
without regrets"), subject to compliance.

## 2. Goal → metrics (pick 1 primary)
| Goal | Primary metric | Supporting metrics | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leads / enquiries | messages, calls, form fills from content | profile visits, link clicks | "Message us on WhatsApp" |
| Sales | tracked sales (codes/UTM) | saves, shares, link clicks | offer + link |
| Awareness | reach of non-followers, follows per 1k views | % viewed, rewatches | follow |
| Trust / education | saves, shares, comments with questions | watch time | follow / ask a question |
Set a baseline (week 0) and a modest 30/60-day target. No promises of results to the client.

## 3. Content pillars (3–5) and the mix
Each pillar: purpose, example topics, proof sources, CTA. Typical mix for a business account:
- 40–60% **educational answers** (the audience's real questions; builds trust and reach)
- 20–30% **"how it works / behind the scenes"** (process, quality, expertise)
- 10–20% **offer / product** (clear, compliant, with CTA)
- 0–10% **proof** (approved testimonials with consent, certifications, community)
Adjust from research; regulated sectors lean educational and disease-awareness, not product claims.

## 4. Series (rotate every 6–8 videos)
A series = one recurring question format + one visual device + one playlist. Example shapes:
"The 60-second answer", "Myth vs fact", "What happens when…", "Price of X explained", "Inside our
<process>". Each new series refreshes the hook and the look so content never feels templated. Each
series finale teases the next one.

## 5. Formats and cadence
- Default: 9:16 Shorts/Reels/TikToks, 25–50 s, one idea each, word-synced kinetic type + animated
  pictograms in the brand system. Optional 1:1 and 16:9 cut-downs.
- Cadence: what the client can approve and sustain. Start 3–5/week; daily only with a 7-video buffer.
- Production rhythm: scripts in **batches of 5–7**; voices recorded/generated per batch; one video
  built per automation run.

## 6. The calendar (`CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv`)
Columns: date, slot (local time + tz), platform(s), content_id, pillar, series, working_title,
central_question, hook_pattern, cta, claims_status, compliance_status, voice_status, production_state,
approval_status, notes. Rules:
- Never schedule a video whose claims are unsourced or compliance is not cleared.
- Keep a buffer of ≥ 5 research-ready topics beyond the calendar.
- Mark holidays and the client's promotions. Fill 30 days in detail; days 31–60 as topics only.

## 7. Approval
Present a one-screen summary: positioning line, pillars with % mix, series #1, cadence, the first 10
titles, KPIs. Ask for approval or edits. Log the decision in `DECISIONS.md` with the date.
