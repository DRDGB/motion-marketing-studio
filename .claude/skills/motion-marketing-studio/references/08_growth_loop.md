# Phase 7: Growth loop (weekly)

Output: `analytics/WEEK_<n>.md` + edits to `CONTENT_CALENDAR.csv` / `03_STRATEGY.md` (with a
`DECISIONS.md` entry for any change of direction).

## Inputs (the client exports; you never log in)
- Per video: views, % viewed / average view duration, viewed-vs-swiped (Shorts), likes, comments,
  shares, saves, follows from video, profile visits, link clicks, and the business metric
  (messages, calls, sales with codes/UTMs).
- Ask for CSV/screenshots weekly on a fixed day. Store raw exports in `analytics/raw/` (no personal data).

## The weekly review (one page)
1. Table of last week's videos with the 5 key metrics and the business metric.
2. What worked: top 2 by the primary metric; name the variable that differs (hook pattern, topic,
   length, first frame, CTA, posting time). Hypothesis, not certainty, with small samples.
3. What failed: bottom 2 and the likely cause (weak first 2 s? unclear payoff? wrong audience?).
4. **One or two experiments for next week** (change one variable, keep the rest), with a success
   threshold. Log them in `analytics/EXPERIMENTS.csv`.
5. Calendar changes: promote winning topics/series, retire losers after 3 tries, refill the buffer.
6. Questions from comments → new topics (the audience writes your calendar).

## Rules
- Do not overreact to one video; look at 3–5 per variable.
- Compare like with like (same platform, same posting window, similar age).
- Respect privacy: no names or handles of commenters in reports.
- Report honestly; never inflate. If numbers are flat, say so and propose a concrete fix.
