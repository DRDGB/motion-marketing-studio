## 0. Bottom line (read this first)

1. **Named prescription-product promotion on public social media is RED in every market we checked** (India,
   Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Côte d'Ivoire, EU/UK) and on every platform's paid side for these countries.
   "It's B2B" and "it's LinkedIn" are not exemptions: a public post is public advertising [E-022..E-038, E-078].
2. **RUO peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, retatrutide), "Sexual Wellness" and "unrestricted custom sourcing" are RED**:
   do not produce, mention, show or link to them. Their presence on the same website/brand is the single biggest
   risk to every other video (account bans, ad-account suspension on destination review, reputational damage)
   [E-042..E-047, E-058, E-061..E-062, E-079].
3. **What we can make now (after the client proves its claims, §12):** corporate brand and capability, regulatory and
   quality education with no product names, process explainers using the client's own verified facility. LinkedIn and
   YouTube first; Meta and WhatsApp as secondary; TikTok low priority.
4. **Peptide APIs (semaglutide/tirzepatide/carbetocin)**: AMBER on LinkedIn only, as B2B API supply to licensed
   manufacturers, after IP and licence proof; RED elsewhere.
5. **Paid ads: hold** until the website no longer lists RUO peptides, sexual-wellness products or "order"-style Rx
   CTAs, and the owner approves spend. Google Ads is RED until then.

## 3. India (advertiser's home)

**Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954**
- s.3 bans taking part in publishing any advertisement referring to a drug in terms suggesting its use for (b)
  "maintenance or improvement of the capacity of human beings for sexual pleasure" and (d) any disease in the
  Schedule, which includes **diabetes**, **sexual impotence**, **venereal diseases** (FACT) [E-001].
- s.6 bans **import into or export from India** of any document containing such an advertisement (FACT) [E-002]. So
  a video made in India for African buyers is still caught.
- s.7 penalties: up to 6 months (first conviction), 1 year (subsequent) (FACT) [E-003]. s.14 savings: ads sent
  confidentially to registered medical practitioners; bona fide scientific treatises (FACT) [E-004].
- Implication (INFERENCE): anti-diabetic and sexual-wellness product videos are RED regardless of target country; "s.4
  misleading advertisement" also catches unproven quality claims.

**Drugs Rules 1945 (Schedule H/H1/X)**
- Manufacturing-licence conditions already state: "No advertisement of the drugs specified in Schedule H, Schedule H1
  or Schedule X shall be made except with the previous sanction of the Central Government" (REPORTED, quoted by
  Lexology) [E-005].
- Draft **G.S.R. 861(E), 28 Sep 2026** would extend the prior-sanction rule to sale/distribution licensees (new Rule
  65(22)) - covers exporters/wholesalers once final; still a draft (REPORTED) [E-006].
- Ceftriaxone, ofloxacin, ornidazole, artesunate, sildenafil, tadalafil, dapoxetine are prescription drugs
  (INFERENCE: Schedule H classification to be confirmed per product by the client's RA). Named-product promotion = RED.

**UCPMP 2024** (Department of Pharmaceuticals, notified 12 Mar 2024) (FACT) [E-007..E-010]
- 1.2: no promotion before marketing approval; promotion must match approval terms.
- 2.2: "safe" never unqualified; never "no side effects"; 2.3: "new" not for products generally available > 1 year;
  2.4 comparisons factual, fair, substantiated.
- 3.2 minimum information in any prescriber-directed material; 3.8 date of last review stated.
- 14.4 CEO annual self-declaration of compliance.
- Applicability to export promotion: the code is silent on export markets (INFERENCE: treat it as applying to all
  promotional conduct by an Indian pharma company; conservative) [E-011].

**Export controls and recent enforcement**
- 23 Feb 2025: Health Ministry stop-activity order on Aveo Pharmaceuticals; Export NOCs and licences for all
  tapentadol + carisoprodol combinations withdrawn; Ghana-bound consignment held; **Export NOC checklist to require the
  importing country's product registration certificate or CDSCO approval for all medicines exported** (FACT, PIB)
  [E-012].
- Bellingcat (Apr 2026): >1,400 tapentadol consignments India to West Africa 2023-2025; AFP (May 2026) reports
  continued shipments (REPORTED) [E-013]. Reputational context: India-to-West-Africa unapproved-drug exports are a
  live media story; "custom/unrestricted sourcing" messaging walks straight into it (OPINION).
- Export NOCs for unapproved/banned/new drugs centralised at CDSCO zonal offices from 7 May 2025; Customs Public
  Notice 88/2026 (Nhava Sheva) on NOC-before-licence sequencing, transition reportedly to 30 Sep 2026 (REPORTED)
  [E-014, E-015].

**FDC status (domestic vs export)**
- **Ofloxacin + Ornidazole injection** banned in India under s.26A (notification of 8 Jun 2017) (REPORTED) [E-016].
  Status of the **oral tablet** FDC not verified this run (open) [E-017]. Banned-in-India FDCs can only be exported
  with a product-specific CDSCO export NOC.
- **Nigeria NAFDAC directive NAFDAC-RDD-030-00 (effective 1 Jul 2024)** discontinued registration of 338 FDCs,
  including **Ofloxacin + Ornidazole suspension**, Ofloxacin + Ornidazole + Zinc, several tramadol combinations,
  Paracetamol + Tapentadol and Artesunate + Arteether + Artemether; it does not list Ceftriaxone + Sulbactam or
  dapoxetine combinations (FACT) [E-018].
- **Ceftriaxone + Sulbactam**: widely marketed in India by several firms; CDSCO approval not verified (REPORTED)
  [E-019]. Client to provide approval/permission.
- Rule (INFERENCE): never show or name any FDC in content; if gated sales material ever names one, it needs the
  Indian approval/NOC and the destination registration on file.

**Revised Schedule M (GMP)**
- MSME (< Rs 250 crore turnover) deadline extended to **31 Dec 2025** (GSR 127(E), 11 Feb 2025); late-Dec 2025 reports
  said a further extension was unlikely (REPORTED) [E-020, E-021]. INFERENCE: as of Nov 2026 revised Schedule M applies
  to all units; "Schedule M compliant" may only be claimed with proof (inspection report / licence renewal under the
  revised schedule).

**Is B2B trade promotion of Rx products to licensed buyers allowed on public social media?** **No** (INFERENCE from
the table) [E-078]. Every regime above defines advertising by content and audience reach, not by the advertiser's
intended buyer. A public LinkedIn/YouTube/Facebook post is readable by anyone, so naming Rx products there is
public advertising in Nigeria (online Rx ban), Ghana (lay electronic media), Kenya (POM public ban), South Africa
(practitioner-only), Côte d'Ivoire (Art. 9 blanket ban) and the EU/UK. Professional-only material must sit in gated
channels with the country approvals listed.

Not researched this run (gaps): Tanzania (TMDA), Ethiopia (EFDA), other francophone/lusophone/Arabic markets, Gulf.
Apply the Côte d'Ivoire/EU default (no public product advertising) until checked.

## 5. Peptides: APIs and RUO

**RUO peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, retatrutide) - RED**
- FDA warning letters treat "research use only" labels as non-controlling: 5 letters on 24 Aug 2026 (Peak
  Performance, Royal, NuScience, Peptide Partners, TXP/Tex Peptides); Gram Peptides on 31 Mar 2026 (retatrutide,
  tirzepatide, bacteriostatic water); trackers count 16 such letters 2024-2026, all disregarding the RUO label;
  evidence used: bacteriostatic water, dosing calculators, marketing claims (FACT for the letters' existence; REPORTED
  for the analysis) [E-042, E-043].
- Retatrutide is investigational, unapproved anywhere; Lilly filed six lawsuits and referred > 200 entities to
  regulators (REPORTED) [E-044].
- BPC-157: FDA lists it under "nominated but withdrawn" on the significant-safety-risk page, citing immunogenicity and
  impurity/API characterisation risks and lack of safety data (FACT) [E-045]. An FDA advisory committee (23-24 Jul 2026)
  narrowly supported adding seven peptides incl. BPC-157 and TB-500 to the 503A bulks list, and HHS floated a draft
  enforcement-easing policy for six compounded peptides; neither makes RUO sales lawful (REPORTED) [E-046].
- WADA 2026 Prohibited List: BPC-157 named under S0 (non-approved substances); TB-500 / thymosin-beta-4 listed
  (FACT) [E-047].
- Platforms: Meta bans ads for "unsafe substances, products or supplements" at its discretion [E-054]; Google bans
  "products that have been subject to any government or regulatory action or warning" and non-approved products
  implied to treat disease (BPC-157 not on the example list, but caught by these clauses) [E-061]; YouTube bans
  facilitating access to pharmaceuticals without prescription and may terminate channels for contact details
  [E-058].
- Reputational (OPINION): RUO peptide sales with ≥99% HPLC purity claims sit beside Rx export claims on the same
  brand. Any of our videos that drives traffic to that site inherits the risk. Recommend the client separate or drop
  the RUO line before any campaign (business decision for the owner).

**Peptide APIs (semaglutide, tirzepatide, carbetocin) - AMBER (LinkedIn only)**
- Semaglutide's core Indian patent expired 20 Mar 2026; > 40 Indian firms launching generics (REPORTED) [E-050];
  Delhi HC allowed exports to non-patent countries before expiry (Dec 2025) (REPORTED) [E-051]. Patents remain in many
  other jurisdictions; tirzepatide patent status in India/targets **not verified** (treat as protected) [E-052].
- Counterfeits: WHO Medical Product Alert N°2/2024 (19 Jun 2024) on falsified Ozempic; FDA counterfeit Ozempic
  seizures (Apr and Dec 2025) (FACT) [E-048, E-049]. GLP-1 content attracts consumer and grey-market attention.
- Carbetocin: Rx obstetric (postpartum haemorrhage) peptide; same API-supply rules apply (INFERENCE).

## 8. AI-content disclosure and Remotion licence

- **YouTube** (FACT) [E-066]: disclose realistic AI content (real person saying something they didn't, altered real
  events, realistic scenes that didn't happen) and **AI-generated music that is the main focus**. Not required for
  non-realistic/animated content or production help (scripts, captions, infographics), or for cloning one's own
  voice. Non-disclosure can lead to labels, removal or YPP suspension. Policy for this client (OPINION): tick the box
  whenever a realistic synthetic human voice or face is used (health topic) and whenever AI music is used.
- **TikTok** (FACT-excerpt) [E-067]: labelling is required for AI content with realistic images, audio or video.
- **Meta** (REPORTED, snapshot) [E-068]: "AI info" label auto-applied from C2PA/IPTC metadata; mandatory disclosure
  for social-issue/political ads. Do not strip metadata.
- **LinkedIn / WhatsApp:** no specific disclosure rule found this run (gap). Apply the YouTube realism test.
- **Nigeria ads:** ARCON vetting requires Nigerian voice-over artists for ads targeted at Nigeria (FACT-excerpt) [E-026];
  a synthetic non-Nigerian voice in a paid ad aimed at Nigeria is likely non-compliant (INFERENCE).
- **Never**: synthetic doctors, pharmacists, patients, buyers or testimonials (10_compliance A/B).
- **Remotion** (FACT, local `remotion@4.0.541` LICENSE) [E-069, E-070]: free only for individuals, for-profit orgs
  with **up to 3 employees**, non-profits or evaluation; otherwise a Company License. If the studio delivers finished
  files only, the studio's headcount counts; if the client operates/owns the Remotion project, headcounts combine.
  **Question for the client:** total headcount of anyone who will operate or own the Remotion project (staff +
  freelancers); default = deliver MP4s only.
