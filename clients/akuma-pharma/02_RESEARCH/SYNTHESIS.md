# Research synthesis: AKUMA PHARMA
_2026-11-10 · Phase 2 · sources: A_BUSINESS, B_BRAND, C_AUDIENCE, D_MARKET, E_PLATFORMS · claims in RESEARCH_LEDGER.csv (266 rows)_

## Verdict
**The business can be marketed, but not on the story its website tells today.** The site presents "Akuma
Enterprise Pharma Pvt. Ltd.", a manufacturer founded in 2009 with 200+ staff, WHO-GMP and ISO 9001:2015.
Public records tied to the site's own tax IDs show **AKUMA ENTERPRISES, a partnership** (GST from 2025-11-07,
IEC 2025-11-19). It has one registered place of business, an office cabin in Kharar. The domain was registered on
2026-10-06 (A-002…A-010; the studio checked the domain date and the PAN entity type independently). No certificate
or licence number is published. Two claims are impossible as written: ISO 9001:2015 "since 2011/2012" and a CTO
"PhD from IIT Mumbai" (A-012, A-013, A-020).
→ Until the client proves them in writing, **no heritage, certification, manufacturing, team or "54 countries"
claim may appear in any video.** Our videos would otherwise amplify misleading claims, and buyers' due diligence
would expose them.

## The 3 strongest insights
1. **Trust is the product.** Importers buy documents that pass *their* regulator: legalised CoPP, Zone IVb data and a
   CTD/ACTD dossier. They carry fresh fears about Indian suppliers (Gambia syrups 2022, Coldrif 2025, the BBC
   opioid exposé). The winning content *teaches buyers how to verify a supplier* and then lets Akuma pass that test
   on camera. This only works once Akuma's own proof is real and checkable (C §2, D §4).
2. **The video lane is empty.** Eight comparable exporters post almost no video: old clips with a few hundred views.
   None posts in FR, PT or AR, and none uses animated explainers. Benchmarks (Cipla Africa, Bachem) show that
   explainers and manifestos earn 15K–500K views (D §5–6). Animated, product-free, multilingual explainers suit
   motion design well and are legally the safest format.
3. **Regulatory education is both the demand and the safe zone.** Buyers search for: what a CoPP is, how to verify
   WHO-GMP, Zone IVb, NAFDAC/PPB/FDA Ghana registration steps, CTD vs ACTD and how to import from India (C §4).
   That content is GREEN on LinkedIn, YouTube, Meta and WhatsApp (E §2), while named-product promotion is RED
   everywhere.

## Audience: top 10 questions (C §4)
1. What is a CoPP and who issues it? 2. How do I verify a WHO-GMP certificate? 3. What does Zone IVb mean for
shelf life? 4. NAFDAC registration fees and checklist? 5. NAFDAC import permit / Green Book? 6. PPB Kenya
registration requirements? 7. FDA Ghana registration fees? 8. TMDA / EFDA guidelines? 9. CTD vs ACTD?
10. How do I import medicines from India safely?
Primary segment: **licensed importers and distributors** (Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Côte d'Ivoire/Francophone West
Africa, South Africa). Tender buyers can be reached only through partners: Global Fund needs WHO PQ/SRA/ERP. API
buyers (local manufacturers) are a growing secondary segment. RUO peptide buyers are excluded.
Channels: LinkedIn and WhatsApp first, YouTube for explainers. Languages EN → FR → PT → AR. Posting hypothesis:
08:00–10:00 WAT.

## Risks (ranked)
1. **Misleading-claims exposure**: entity, history, certifications, team. This affects the client's legal position,
   buyers' trust and our studio's reputation.
2. **The website's catalogue**:
   - RUO peptides: BPC-157, TB-500, Retatrutide, MK-677, Melanotan.
   - Nandrolone.
   - A "Herbal Sex Booster" and ED combinations.
   - Third-party brands (Ventolin, Crocin, Becosules, Candid, Mucolite).
   - Legacy steroid/nootropic pages still in the search index.

   Platforms review the destination site, so these put every account and ad account at risk (A §9, E §0).
3. **India's DMR Act** s.3/s.6: sexual-wellness and diabetes adverts are banned, *including for export* (E §3).
4. **Product status**: Ofloxacin+Ornidazole status varies by country (NAFDAC discontinued the suspension in 2024). The
   client must prove Indian approval for every FDC.
5. **Name collision**: AKUMA PHARMA PRIVATE LIMITED (Assam, unrelated) and Akums Drugs. Check the trademark before
   building a brand on "AKUMA PHARMA".
6. **Nigeria**: ARCON vets ads and requires Nigerian voice-over artists, which constrains AI voice there.
7. **Remotion licence**: the client's headcount decides it (free only if ≤ 3 people in total).
