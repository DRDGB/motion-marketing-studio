Commercial Formulation Regulatory Dossiers (eCTD / ACTD)

Akuma Enterprise Pharma maintains comprehensive, pre-compiled regulatory and analytical documentation packages for our WHO-GMP commercial finished formulations, Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs, Research Peptides (RUO), and custom client-requested molecules — serving all 54 African countries & global markets. Engineered specifically for fast-track NRA registration across Africa (NAFDAC Nigeria, PPB Kenya, SAHPRA South Africa, TMDA Tanzania, FDA Ghana, EFDA Ethiopia, WAHO, EAC, SADC ZAZIBONA) alongside GCC, Latin America, Southeast Asia, and CIS markets.

Dossier Package Structure (Modules 1 through 5)

Every commercial product dossier follows harmonized ICH CTD and ASEAN ACTD specifications:

Module 1: Administrative

Application forms, comprehensive Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (COPP / WHO-Format), Free Sale Certificate (FSC), GMP Certificate, and proposed Product Information / Leaflets.

Module 2: Summaries

Overall Quality Summary (QOS), Clinical Overview, Non-Clinical Overview, and tabular summaries prepared by certified regulatory affairs specialists.

Module 3: Quality (CMC)

Complete drug substance specifications, manufacturing process validation, batch analysis reports (CoAs), analytical method validation, and container-closure system integrity.

Stability: Zone IVb

Accelerated (40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH) and real-time long-term (30°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH) stability testing demonstrating 24 to 36 months commercial shelf life in tropical zones.

African National Regulatory Authority (NRA) Filing Matrix & Sea/Air Corridors

West Africa (NAFDAC · FDA Ghana · WAHO)

COPP, Free Sale Certificate, NAFDAC NAPAMS dossier readiness, pre-shipment clean report of inspection & analysis (CRIA) support, and French/English ECOWAS packs.

East Africa (PPB · TMDA · NDA · EFDA)

Southern & North Africa (SAHPRA · EDA · DMP)
