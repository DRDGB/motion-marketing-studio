eCTD & ACTD Regulatory Dossiers (Modules 1–5) for Africa & Global Registrations

Eliminate registration bottlenecks across National Regulatory Authorities (NRAs). Akuma Enterprise Pharma provides audit-validated 5-Module ACTD and eCTD technical dossiers for our finished formulations, Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs, and custom export lines.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma supplies turnkey 5-Module ACTD and eCTD regulatory dossiers, WHO-Format Certificates of Pharmaceutical Product (CoPP), Free Sale Certificates (FSC), batch CoAs, and 24–36 month ICH Zone IVb stability studies tailored for African NRAs (NAFDAC Nigeria, PPB Kenya, SAHPRA South Africa, TMDA Tanzania, FDA Ghana, EFDA, NDA, WAHO, ZAZIBONA) and global health ministries.
5-Module ACTD & eCTD Architecture
Module 1 (Administrative): WHO-GMP certificates, Manufacturing Licences, CoPP, FSC, Letter of Authorization (LoA), SmPC, PIL, and EN/FR/PT/AR artworks.
Module 2 (Quality Overall Summary): QOS-PD, Clinical & Non-Clinical Overviews.
Module 3 (Quality / CMC): Drug substance controls, manufacturing process validation (3 commercial batches), analytical method validation, container-closure extractables, and stability reports.
Module 4 & 5 (Non-Clinical / Bioequivalence): Biowaiver / BE dissolution profiles (multimedia pH 1.2, 4.5, 6.8) and literature safety reports.
ICH Zone IVb Climatic Stability Validation

African, MENA, and tropical LATAM markets require Zone IVb climatic data. Our stability chambers conduct real-time studies at 30°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH and accelerated studies at 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH supporting full 24–36 month export shelf lives.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do your dossiers match NAFDAC, PPB, SAHPRA, TMDA, and WAHO templates?

Yes. We compile dossiers directly in regional CTD / ACTD / ZAZIBONA / EAC joint assessment formats with country-specific Module 1 checklists and bilingual EN/FR/PT texts.

How quickly are dossier inspection packs issued to qualified importers?

Dossier Letters of Access (LoA), QOS summaries, CoPP samples, and HPLC/MS CoAs are issued within 24–48 hours, with full 5-module electronic trees dispatched inside 3–5 business days.

Are dissolution curves and Certificate of Analysis included for antimalarials and anti-diabetics?

Yes. Complete multimedia dissolution profiles (f2 similarity factors) and batch CoAs accompany solid oral dossiers.
