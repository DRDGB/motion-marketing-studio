Finished Pharmaceutical Formulation Exporter from India to Africa & Global Markets

Akuma Enterprise Pharma Pvt. Ltd. exports WHO-GMP commercial finished human medicines, lyophilized peptides, and custom-sourced molecules from Asia to all 54 African countries and global licensed importers, ministries of health, hospital tender boards, and institutional buyers across Africa and worldwide.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma is an Indian pharmaceutical exporter exporting from Asia to all 54 African countries alongside MENA, LATAM, and CIS markets — showcasing finished formulations and lyophilized peptides while fulfilling every buyer request for custom molecules and tender specs alongside MENA, LATAM, and CIS markets with Zone IVb stability and ACTD/eCTD dossiers.
Africa Priority & Global Freight Corridors

Dispatched via Mumbai Air Cargo (BOM), Nhava Sheva / JNPT Sea Port, and New Delhi Air Cargo (DEL) with validated cold-chain and tropical dry freight:

West Africa (ECOWAS/WAHO): Nigeria (Apapa / Tin Can / Lagos LOS), Ghana (Tema / Accra ACC), Cote d'Ivoire (Abidjan), Senegal (Dakar), Cameroon (Douala).
East Africa (EAC & Horn): Kenya (Mombasa / Nairobi NBO), Tanzania (Dar es Salaam), Uganda (Entebbe / Kampala), Ethiopia (Addis Ababa / Djibouti corridor), Rwanda (Kigali).
Southern & Lusophone Africa (SADC/ZAZIBONA): South Africa (Durban / JNB), Zambia, Zimbabwe, Angola (Luanda), Mozambique (Maputo).
North Africa, MENA, LATAM & Global: Egypt, Morocco, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Brazil, Mexico, Europe, and APAC.
Zone IVb Climate Protection & Cold Chain

High ambient heat and humidity across Sub-Saharan Africa demand purpose-built packaging. We utilize cold-form Alu-Alu blister laminates, amber Type I borosilicate vials for Artesunate and sterile antibiotics, child-resistant amber PET bottles, and EPS thermal shippers with continuous temperature loggers for peptides and cold-chain parenterals.

Frequently Asked Questions
Which international Incoterms do you support for Africa & global orders?

We supply FOB (JNPT/BOM/DEL), CIF, CFR, CIP (Air Cargo), and Ex-Works (EXW) tailored to each importer's customs and inland transit structure.

Are your formulations tested for African Zone IVb tropical stability?

Yes. Real-time (30°C ± 2°C / 75% RH) and accelerated (40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH) ICH Q1A Zone IVb stability data back 24–36 month shelf lives.

What payment terms are available for institutional and distributor imports?

We accept T/T international wire transfers (USD, EUR, GBP), Irrevocable Letters of Credit (LC at Sight), and approved institutional credit terms for established buyers.
