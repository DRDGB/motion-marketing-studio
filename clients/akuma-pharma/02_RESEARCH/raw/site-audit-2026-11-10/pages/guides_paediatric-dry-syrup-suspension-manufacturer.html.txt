Paediatric Dry Syrup, Oral Suspension & Infant Drops Manufacturer

Child-safe oral liquid and reconstitutable formulations engineered with taste-masking technology, child-resistant closures, and calibrated dosing pipettes for African and global community clinics.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma supplies paediatric dry syrups, oral suspensions, and calibrated infant drops—including Cefixime & Cefpodoxime dry syrups, Chloroquine Phosphate 160mg/10ml suspension, Paracetamol 100mg/ml infant drops, and WHO Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS)—with taste-masking polymers and ICH Zone IVb tropical stability.
Palatability, Dosing Accuracy & Tropical Moisture Barriers

High paediatric compliance depends on accurate reconstitution and palatable masking of bitter antibiotics and antimalarials. Our granules are compacted in moisture-tight amber bottles with embossed water-fill rings, child-resistant ROPP caps, calibrated 10 ml dosing cups, and glass dropper pipettes.

Paediatric & Oral Liquid Portfolio
Antimalarial & Anti-Infective: Chloroquine Phosphate 160 mg/10 ml Suspension (100 ml), Cefixime 50mg/100mg Dry Syrup, Cefpodoxime 50mg/100mg Dry Syrup.
Paediatric Analgesic & Colic Drops: Paracetamol 100 mg/ml Baby Drops, Dicyclomine Drops, Cyproheptadine appetite-stimulant drops.
GI & Respiratory Oral Liquids: Sucralfate, Magaldrate + Simethicone + Oxetacaine, Dextromethorphan/Ambroxol cough syrups, and WHO-ORS electrolytes.
Frequently Asked Questions
How long do reconstituted dry syrups maintain potency?

Once reconstituted with boiled and cooled water, our Cefixime and Cefpodoxime dry syrups maintain assay potency for 7–14 days under label storage instructions.

Are calibrated measuring devices included in every carton?

Yes. Unit cartons include either a graduated PP measuring cup/spoon or calibrated dropper pipette with multilingual usage pictograms.

How are oral liquids protected against humidity during African sea freight?

Bottles feature induction heat seals, desiccant-protected closures where required, and Zone IVb validated stability up to 30°C/75% RH.
