PCD Pharma Manufacturing & Exclusive Distributor Supply Partner

Build high-margin regional distribution with Akuma Enterprise Pharma's WHO-GMP finished formulations & custom export portfolio spanning Antimalarials, Antibiotics, Anti-Diabetics, Sexual Wellness & Urology, PPIs, Dermatology, and Paediatric medicines.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma supplies licensed pharma distributors and regional franchise partners with high-turnover WHO-GMP finished formulations across 15 therapeutic sectors, complete promotional visuals, reliable stock availability, and dedicated territory protection across India and export markets.
Distributor & Franchise Advantages
High-Turnover Portfolio & Custom Sourcing: Showcase finished formulations & on-demand strengths including Artesunate IV, Artemether-Lumefantrine, Sildenafil/Tadalafil/Dapoxetine combos, Dapagliflozin/Glimepiride combos, Cefixime, Pantoprazole, and Terbinafine.
Territory Protection: Structured regional exclusivity for qualified license holders and national importers.
Complete Regulatory & Marketing Support: Technical spec sheets, batch CoAs, digital visual assets, and fast stock replenishment.
High-Demand Therapeutic Lines
Antimalarials: Artesunate 30–240 mg IV, Artemether ampoules, α-β Arteether 150 mg, Artemether 80/Lumefantrine 480 mg ACT tablets, Chloroquine.
Sexual Wellness & Urology: Dapoxetine + Sildenafil/Tadalafil, Sildenafil 50/100 mg, Tadalafil 10/20 mg, Vardenafil, Silodosin, Tamsulosin + Dutasteride.
Anti-Diabetic & Anti-Infectives: Dapagliflozin + Metformin ER, Glimepiride + Pioglitazone + Metformin, Ceftriaxone-Sulbactam, Cefixime dry syrups.
Frequently Asked Questions
What documents are required to become an authorized distributor?

Domestic partners require a valid Drug License and GST; international importers require a valid National Regulatory Authority (NRA) import license or Ministry of Health authorization.

Can we request molecules or strengths not listed in the online catalog?

Yes. Beyond our featured online formulations, our export desk takes every inquiry and manufactures or sources custom molecules, dosages, and pack formats on demand.

Can distributors combine multiple categories in a single export consignment?

Yes. Mixed-category air or sea consolidations across tablets, capsules, sterile vials, syrups, and topicals are routinely supported.
