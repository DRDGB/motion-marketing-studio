India-to-Africa & Global Pharmaceutical Export Logistics

Bridging WHO-GMP Indian manufacturing with high-reliability healthcare supply across West Africa, East Africa, Southern Africa, Central Africa, North Africa, MENA, LATAM, Europe, and North America.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma exports WHO-GMP finished medicines, peptides & custom buyer-requested molecules from Asia across all 54 countries of Africa (West, East, Southern, Central, and North Africa) alongside GCC, LATAM, CIS, EU, and North America via Mumbai (BOM/JNPT) and Delhi (DEL).
Regional Regulatory & Cold-Chain Playbooks
Sub-Saharan & North Africa: Priority stock in Artesunate IV, Artemether-Lumefantrine, Cefixime dry syrup, Ceftriaxone-Sulbactam, anti-diabetics, and sexual wellness generics aligned with NAFDAC, PPB, SAHPRA, TMDA, FDA Ghana, EFDA, and WAHO.
GCC & MENA: Sterile parenterals, ophthalmic drops, and Peptide APIs aligned with SFDA/MOHAP dossier specs.
LATAM & CIS: Bilingual ES/PT/RU leaflets, ANVISA/COFEPRIS documentation, and temperature-monitored air/sea freight.
Hospital Tenders & NGO Supply Readiness

We partner with licensed national importers and NGO procurement agencies (supporting public hospital tenders and private pharmacy chains) with factory-direct pricing, institutional tender packs, CRIAM/GS1 barcode options, and validated air/sea transit schedules.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do you deliver to landlocked African nations such as Uganda, Rwanda, Ethiopia, Zambia, and Zimbabwe?

We utilize priority air cargo (DEL/BOM to NBO/ADD/KGL/EBB/LUN/HRE) and bonded corridor sea-land transit via Mombasa, Dar es Salaam, Djibouti, Durban, and Beira.

Do you offer bulk hospital tender pack sizes alongside commercial pharmacy packs?

Yes. Standard retail Alu-Alu cartons (e.g., 10x10) and bulk institutional hospital jars/combi-boxes are both supported.

What customs export documents ship with every consignment?

Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Chamber Certificate of Origin, Airway Bill / Bill of Lading, WHO CoPP/FSC, Batch CoA, and Cold-Chain Data Logger printout (when applicable).
