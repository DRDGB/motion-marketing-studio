Frequently Asked Questions
How do you separate Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs from Research Peptides (RUO)?

Every product clearly displays its classification: AKP-API items are Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs for licensed drug manufacturers with ICH Q7 / CoA documentation, whereas AKP-RP items carry explicit Research Use Only (RUO — Not for Human or Veterinary Consumption) banners and labeling. Custom syntheses follow the same strict regulatory classification.

What analytical QC reports ship with each peptide batch?

Every peptide vial batch includes reverse-phase HPLC purity verification (>=99.0%), ESI-MS molecular weight confirmation, moisture/TFA/acetate content where applicable, and a 16-section GHS SDS.

How are lyophilized peptides shipped to Africa and global research labs?

Peptides ship as temperature-stabilized lyophilized vials in validated insulated cold-chain air parcels via DEL/BOM express air cargo.
