Sterile Injectable, Artesunate & Cephalosporin Dry Powder Vials Manufacturer

Critical-care aseptic parenterals manufactured under WHO-GMP for African and global hospital tenders: full-range Artesunate IV/IM lyophilized vials (30–240 mg), Artemether/Arteether ampoules, and beta-lactamase cephalosporin dry powder vials with WFI.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma manufactures sterile dry powder injection vials and USP Type I glass liquid ampoules under WHO-GMP, covering Artesunate 30 mg to 240 mg IV/IM antimalarial vials, Artemether 20/40/80 mg & α-β Arteether 150 mg ampoules, Ceftriaxone, Cefoperazone + Sulbactam, Piperacillin-Tazobactam, and Amikacin for hospital tenders across Africa and global markets.
ISO Class 5 Aseptic Cleanrooms & Combi-Packs

Parenteral safety requires zero-compromise sterility assurance (SAL 10-6). Our sterile suites utilize 300°C depyrogenation tunnels, Grade A laminar filling, automated stoppering/capping, 100% optical particulate inspection, and combi-pack integration with Sterile Water for Injection (WFI) or sodium bicarbonate diluent ampoules for Artesunate.

Hospital-Critical Injectable Lineup
Antimalarial Parenterals: Artesunate 30, 60, 90, 120, 150, 180, 210, 240 mg lyophilized dry powder vials; Artemether 20/40/80 mg & α-β Arteether 150 mg/2ml ampoules.
Antibacterial Dry Vials: Ceftriaxone (250mg–1g), Ceftriaxone + Sulbactam/Tazobactam, Cefoperazone + Sulbactam, Piperacillin + Tazobactam, Meropenem.
Liquid Ampoules: Amikacin 100/250/500 mg, Diclofenac 75mg/1ml, Methylcobalamin Multi-B, Nandrolone Decanoate.
Frequently Asked Questions
Do Artesunate and cephalosporin dry powder vials include Sterile Water for Injection (WFI)?

Yes. Hospital combi-packs are supplied with matching WFI / diluent ampoules inside unit boxes backed by COA, sterility, and LAL bacterial endotoxin test reports.

What glass container type is used for parenteral vials and ampoules?

USP Type I neutral borosilicate glass vials and amber/clear ampoules are used across sterile parenterals to prevent alkaline leaching in Zone IVb climates.

What sterility and endotoxin tests are executed before release?

Every batch undergoes 14-day USP/BP/IP sterility testing, LAL bacterial endotoxin quantification, sub-visible particulate matter testing (USP &lt;788&gt;), and vacuum/dye leak integrity checks.
