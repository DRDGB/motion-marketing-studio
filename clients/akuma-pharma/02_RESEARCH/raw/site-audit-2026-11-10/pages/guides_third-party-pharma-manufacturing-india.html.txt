Third-Party & Contract Pharmaceutical Manufacturing in India

Scale your pharmaceutical brand with Akuma Enterprise Pharma's WHO-GMP third-party manufacturing solutions across validated finished formulations and custom client formulations, bilingual EN/FR/PT/AR packaging, and turnkey ACTD/eCTD regulatory dossiers.

Direct Answer Summary: Third-party pharma manufacturing by Akuma Enterprise Pharma lets licensed pharmaceutical marketers and African/global importers source private-label finished dosage forms from a WHO-GMP facility in India across solid, liquid, sterile injectable &amp; peptide dosage forms (catalog &amp; custom client formulas) with commercial MOQs starting from 1,000 packs, multilingual Alu-Alu/blister artwork, and 5-Module ACTD/eCTD dossiers.
Why Importers & Brands Partner with Akuma Enterprise Pharma

Contract manufacturing requires speed, dossier completeness, and tropical climate durability:

Flexible Commercial MOQs: Commercial minimum order quantities starting at 1,000 packs per finished formulation (and 10–50 vials for lyophilized peptides).
Africa & Bilingual Packaging: Custom cartons, Alu-Alu moisture-barrier blisters, English/French/Portuguese/Arabic leaflets (PIL), and CRIAM/GS1 traceability readiness.
Rapid Dispatch: Repeat orders processed within 15–25 business days via Mumbai (BOM/JNPT) or New Delhi (DEL).
Strict Confidentiality: NDAs, brand exclusivity, and turnkey Letter of Access (LoA) regulatory support.
Third-Party Manufacturing Process Flow
Formulation Selection: Select from showcased finished formulations or submit your own target molecule, strength, and pack size.
Quotation & Dossier Scope: Receive CIF/FOB pricing plus ACTD/eCTD dossier availability within 24 hours.
Multilingual Artwork Approval: Finalize national NRA-compliant labels and tropical barrier packaging.
Manufacturing & Batch QC: Assay (HPLC/UV), dissolution, uniformity, sterility/LAL testing for parenterals, and Zone IVb stability documentation.
Air/Sea Export & Release: Shipped with batch CoA, CoPP, FSC, and CoO for rapid port clearance.
Frequently Asked Questions
What are the MOQs for third-party contract manufacturing?

Commercial finished formulations typically start at 1,000 to 2,500 packs per SKU; Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs start at 50 vials and RUO Research Peptides at 10 vials.

Can you customize labels in French, Portuguese, Swahili, or Arabic for African markets?

Yes. Our regulatory artwork team adapts cartons, foils, and patient information leaflets (RCP/Notice) for WAHO/ECOWAS Francophone, Anglophone, Lusophone (Angola/Mozambique), and North African countries.

What analytical testing accompanies each contract batch?

Every batch includes HPLC/UV assay, related substances, dissolution, weight variation, and full sterility/LAL endotoxin certificates for sterile dry powder and liquid injectables.
