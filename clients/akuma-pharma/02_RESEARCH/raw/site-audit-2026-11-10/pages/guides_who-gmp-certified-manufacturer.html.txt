WHO-GMP Certified Pharmaceutical Formulation & Peptide Manufacturer in India

Akuma Enterprise Pharma Pvt. Ltd. is an audit-ready, WHO-GMP and ISO 9001:2015 certified Indian pharmaceutical manufacturer and exporter exporting WHO-GMP commercial finished dosage formulations, Peptide APIs, Research Peptides (RUO), and custom buyer-requested molecules across all 54 African countries and global healthcare systems.

Direct Answer Summary: Akuma Enterprise Pharma is a WHO-GMP certified pharmaceutical manufacturer and exporter headquartered in SAS Nagar, Punjab, India. The company exports from Asia to all 54 countries of Africa and worldwide—showcasing commercial finished human medicines across key dosage forms alongside Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs and Research Peptides (RUO), while handling every custom molecule, dosage, or tender specification requested by buyers with complete eCTD/ACTD dossiers and ICH Zone IVb stability.
WHO-GMP Compliance & Manufacturing Infrastructure

Operating in SAS Nagar, Punjab, our manufacturing suites are engineered to meet stringent WHO-GMP guidelines, Schedule M standards, and IP, BP, USP, and EP pharmacopoeial requirements. Cleanroom HVAC filtration, pressure differentials, and online particulate monitoring ensure consistent export-grade quality.

From high-speed rotary tablet compression and tropical Alu-Alu blistering to sterile isolator lyophilization for cephalosporins, antimalarial Artesunate vials, and high-purity peptides (≥99.0% HPLC + ESI-MS), every commercial lot ships with CoA, batch records, and stability data.

Complete 12-Category & 15-Sector Capabilities

Akuma Enterprise Pharma showcases commercial finished formulations & peptides across core therapeutic sectors, while fulfilling custom manufacturing & sourcing for any molecule requested:

Oral Solids: Film-coated, enteric-coated, and dispersible tablets, plus multi-particulate sustained-release capsules across Antimalarials, Anti-Diabetic, Sexual Wellness & Urology, Antibiotics, PPIs, and NSAIDs.
Sterile Parenterals: Lyophilized Artesunate IV/IM vials (30–240 mg) and cephalosporin dry powder vials with WFI alongside Artemether, Arteether, and Amikacin liquid ampoules.
Peptides (APIs, RUO & Custom): Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide, Liraglutide, Carbetocin, Oxytocin, Leuprorelin), strictly labeled Research Peptides (RUO: Retatrutide, BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu), and custom sequence synthesis.
Topicals, Eye/Ear, Paediatric & Hospital Care: Dermatology creams, sterile ophthalmic/otic drops, metered nasal sprays, taste-masked paediatric dry syrups, infant drops, oral liquids, and surgical sanitizers.
Frequently Asked Questions
What standards do your WHO-GMP facilities adhere to?

Our commercial formulations adhere to WHO Good Manufacturing Practices (WHO-GMP), Schedule M, ISO 9001:2015, and IP/BP/USP/EP compendial monographs, with Zone IVb (30°C/75% RH & 40°C/75% RH) tropical stability validation.

Can you supply a Certificate of Pharmaceutical Product (COPP)?

Yes. We provide WHO-format Certificates of Pharmaceutical Product (CoPP) and Free Sale Certificates (FSC) alongside 5-Module ACTD/eCTD dossiers for NAFDAC, PPB, SAHPRA, TMDA, FDA Ghana, EFDA, and global registrations.

What is the typical production lead time for export orders?

In-stock export orders ship in 1–2 weeks by air cargo (BOM/DEL) or scheduled sea freight; custom third-party production batches run 21–35 calendar days with full batch CoA.
