## What We Do

At Akuma Enterprise Pharma, we export from Asia across all 54 African countries and global healthcare markets — showcasing WHO-GMP commercial finished formulations, Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs, and lyophilized Research Peptides (RUO), while fulfilling every client request for custom molecules, strengths, and packs.

Providing turnkey regulatory support with complete 5-Module eCTD and ACTD dossiers, Bioequivalence (BE) study summaries, and long-term Zone IVb stability validation data to enable fast-track product registration and import authorization with national regulatory agencies across Africa, Southeast Asia, Middle East, and Latin America.

Managing end-to-end pharmaceutical cold-chain and export logistics, Certificate of Analysis (CoA) batch verification, validated tertiary packaging, and continuous pharmacovigilance to ensure commercial drug supplies reach healthcare providers safely without supply chain interruption.

Ensuring uncompromising compliance with WHO-GMP and ISO 9001:2015 standards through rigorous in-process quality controls, high-speed automated blistering and vial filling lines, cleanroom environmental monitoring, and comprehensive analytical release testing.
