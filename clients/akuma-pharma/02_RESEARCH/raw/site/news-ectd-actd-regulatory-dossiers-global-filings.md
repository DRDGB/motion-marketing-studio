# eCTD & ACTD Regulatory Dossier Library Launched for Fast-Track Africa & Global Filings
September 22, 2026 · 1 min read
"To accelerate international market access for our distributor partners, Akuma Enterprise Pharma has finalized ready-to-file regulatory dossiers across our commercial finished formulations, Pharmaceutical Peptide APIs, and custom client filings across all therapeutic sectors. Each dossier package contains Module 1 administrative data, Module 2 quality summaries, and complete Module 3 CMC sections with 24-month Zone IVb stability testing."
## Standardized CTD & ACTD Structure — "...eliminate submission friction with African ministries of health including NAFDAC, PPB, SAHPRA, TMDA, and FDA Ghana."
## Immediate Availability — "Distributor partners can request access tokens and Letters of Access (LoA) directly from our regulatory desk."
