## 1. The big idea

### India is far. Your team there isn't.
Every pharmaceutical importer in Africa knows the gap: the medicines are made in India, the business is run in Lagos, Accra,
Nairobi or Dakar, and between them sit an ocean, several time zones and a stack of documents. Buyers have learned to be wary of
promises from far away (D-051, C_AUDIENCE §2).

**The campaign closes that gap on screen, one video at a time.** Akuma is the team in India that sources from WHO-GMP
certified partner manufacturers, checks the paperwork and stays with the order all the way to the buyer's door (SP-1…SP-6).
We never shout "trust us". We show the work, and the distance gets shorter with every frame.

**Campaign line:** **"Closer Than You Think."** FR: **« Plus proche que vous ne le pensez. »**

### The creative platform: *The Thread*
One crimson thread (the brand's single accent, B_BRAND) runs through every film:
- it starts at a partner manufacturer in India, passes through each check and document, crosses a drawn ocean, and ends tied
  to the buyer's door;
- it is the opening sting, the transition device and the end-card knot;
- it turns the abstract service ("sourcing partner") into a picture anyone understands in one second, with the sound off,
  on a phone, in any language.

**Visual world:**
- Two worlds, light and dark (#FAFAFB paper, #0D1117 night ocean), joined by the crimson thread.
- Playfair Display headlines with **one crimson italic word**, Plus Jakarta Sans for everything else.
- Drawn, unlabelled cartons, blisters, documents, ports, planes and maps. Diagrams instead of stock footage.
- Calm motion that is never alarmist.

### Manifesto (launch film voice-over, ~40 s, AKV-001)
> You run a pharmaceutical business. Your shelves serve your city.
> The medicines you need are made in India: an ocean, three time zones and a stack of documents away.
> So we made India closer.
> We find WHO-GMP certified partner manufacturers. We check their licences and documents. We bring you the manufacturer's
> batch certificate with every shipment. And our team stays with your order, from sourcing in India to your door.
> One thread. From their factory to your door.
> **Akuma. Closer than you think.**

(SP-1…SP-6. FR version written natively, not translated word for word.)

## 3. Positioning and message

### 3.1 Positioning
For **licensed pharmaceutical importers, distributors, wholesalers, pharmacy chains and hospital procurement teams in
Africa** (C_AUDIENCE §1) **who need dependable supply from India with documents their regulator accepts** (C-024, C-035,
C-036, C-057), **Akuma is their team in India**. Akuma sources finished medicines from WHO-GMP certified partner manufacturers,
checks their licences and documents, and assists from sourcing all the way to delivery at the buyer's door (SP-1…SP-6).
**Proof the buyer can check:**
- certificates are issued in the manufacturer's name and can be verified with the issuing authority (SP-6, C-013);
- the manufacturer's batch CoA travels with every shipment (SP-4);
- supply goes only to licensed buyers, where the product is registered or authorised (SP-5).

- **One line:** **"Your team in India: from sourcing to your door."** FR « Votre équipe en Inde : du sourcing jusqu'à votre porte. »
- **Channel promise:** "Short, clear answers to what importers need to know before sourcing medicines from India, and a team
  that takes it from there." FR « Des réponses courtes et claires pour importer depuis l'Inde, et une équipe qui s'occupe du reste. »

### 3.2 Core message (owner's idea, campaign-ready)
**EN:** "You run a pharmaceutical business and you're looking for a place to source your products? We have a solution. Akuma
brings India closer. We assist you from sourcing in India all the way to delivery at your door. Our team handles it."
**FR:** « Vous dirigez une entreprise pharmaceutique et cherchez où vous approvisionner ? Nous avons la solution. Akuma rapproche
l'Inde de vous : nous vous accompagnons du sourcing en Inde jusqu'à la livraison à votre porte. Notre équipe s'en occupe. »
(SP-1…SP-3)

**The anti-repetition rule:** the full core message appears **at most once every 6 videos** (launch, series finales). Every other
video expresses **one facet** of it (distance, documents, checks, delivery, honesty, time), each through a different emotion,
hook and story shape (§6). The message adds up across the feed without being repeated.

### 3.3 Message house
- **Roof:** Your team in India: from sourcing to your door.
- **Four pillars** (the "relay"):
  - **Find:** WHO-GMP certified partner manufacturers in India (SP-1).
  - **Check:** licences and documents reviewed (SP-2).
  - **Document:** the manufacturer's batch CoA, plus CoPP, stability data and dossier where they exist (SP-4).
  - **Deliver:** export and delivery to your door (SP-3, A-026).
- **Foundation:** "We don't make medicines. We make sourcing them from India simple." (SP-6), licensed buyers only (SP-5).

## 5. Funnel architecture

| Stage | Share | Pillars | Job | Hand-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Attention** | ~30% | P3 Closer, P4 Verify, topical moments | Stop the scroll of a pharma business owner; make India feel close | Follow + next episode → profile → website home / How we work |
| **Understanding** | ~55% | P1 Import Answers (S1), P2 How We Work (S2) | Answer real questions (C-048); show the relay from India to the door | Website guides, partner documents page, Importer's Checklist |
| **Action** | ~15% | P5 Talk to Our Team | Make the first message effortless | WhatsApp prefill · Request a quote |

**Rules for every video:**
- The payoff lands before the CTA. There is **one CTA**, matched to the stage, and a "next" teaser.
- The caption carries a UTM short link, and the LinkedIn first comment carries the same link.
- Education videos always point to **one deeper page**, and that page ends with *Request a quote* and the WhatsApp button.
  Learning turns into leads on the site.
- Hard CTAs appear at most once in every 3 videos, so the feed stays generous.

### 6.4 Visual metaphors (motion design)
- **The crimson thread** from a factory in India to the buyer's door. This is the campaign device.
- **The bridge built plank by plank:** each plank is one check.
- **The relay baton:** partner manufacturer → Akuma's team → freight → your door. It makes "who does what" clear (SP-6).
- **The document stack collapsing into one folder.**
- **Clocks across time zones** rotating into one shared working day (C-054).
- **Puzzle pieces:** product, manufacturer, documents and delivery click together.
- **The batch passport:** each carton carries its papers and gets stamped along the route. All stamps are generic.
- **The question card** unfolding into its answer (series #1).
- **Two climates, two chambers:** gauges rising to 30 °C / 75% RH (C-036).
- **The key and lock:** the right document opens the next step.
- **The door:** every journey ends at a door that opens to light. This is the brand's arrival moment.

### 6.7 WhatsApp (opt-in only)
- **Channel:** viewers follow through the website, LinkedIn and email signatures. Post each video with one line and the
  website link, up to 3 a week.
- **Status:** a 30-s cut of each video for contacts who saved the number.
- **Never:** unsolicited bulk messages, bought lists, or adding people to groups. No catalogue, cart or prices for medicines,
  because WhatsApp Business commerce is prohibited for drugs (E-063). Status and Channel carry service and education only.
- **Forwardable files:** light (720p, target under 10 MB) and legible from the first frame, because buyers forward in groups on
  metered data (C_AUDIENCE §3).
