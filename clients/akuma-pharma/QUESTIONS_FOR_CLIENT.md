# Questions for AKUMA PHARMA (intake + evidence request)
_Prepared 2026-11-10 by the studio after researching the website and public records. Send as one message.
Answers go into 01_CLIENT_BRIEF.md; documents are logged in DECISIONS.md._

We have studied your website, your market, your competitors and the advertising rules in India and your
African markets. To plan videos that build buyers' trust and keep your accounts safe, we need the items below.
Part A is about how you want to work with us. Part B is the evidence we need before any claim about your company
can appear in a video.

## A. Intake (about your goals and setup)
1. **Your role and contact:** who is our day-to-day contact, and who has final approval?
2. **Social accounts:** do you have LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X or a WhatsApp Business
   catalogue? Send the links. If you have none, may we prepare (not create) a setup plan for LinkedIn and YouTube?
3. **Goal for the next 60 days**, pick one: qualified importer enquiries · dossier requests · distributor
   partnerships in specific countries · brand awareness before a trade event. Any numeric target?
4. **Priority countries** (top 3–5) and **languages** (EN, FR, PT, AR?).
5. **Brand assets:** official logo (SVG/AI/EPS) and which "A" mark is current; any brand guide; your **own** photos
   or video of facilities, packing and dispatch (not stock).
6. **Voice:** AI voice (which provider and plan?), your own team member's recorded voice, or captions only?
   Note: Nigeria requires Nigerian voice-over artists for adverts.
7. **Approval and publishing:** who reviews scripts (we need a named **Head of Regulatory Affairs / responsible
   pharmacist**), how fast, and who presses "publish"?
8. **Budget/limits:** paid tools (voice plan, music library), possible paid ads later, posting frequency you can sustain.
9. **Events:** will you attend the Pharmexcil buyer-seller meets (Nairobi, Accra, Luanda, 30 Nov–8 Dec 2026), CPHI
   or Africa Health? Videos can be timed to them.
10. **Calls to action:** where should buyers go: the WhatsApp desk, "Request a dossier", email, or a landing page?

## B. Evidence (required before we state anything about the company)
1. **Legal entity:** is the contracting entity AKUMA ENTERPRISES (partnership, GSTIN 03ACLFA6940J1ZB)? Does
   "Akuma Enterprise Pharma Pvt. Ltd." exist? If yes, send its CIN. How do akumaenterprises.in and
   akumaenterprises.com relate to you?
2. **History and figures:** what supports "founded 2009", "15+ years", "200+ employees", "54 countries served",
   "zero recalls" and "100% inspections passed"? Send evidence, or agree that we recommend removing them.
3. **Manufacturing:** do you own a plant? If not, name your contract or loan-licence manufacturers per product line,
   with their manufacturing licence numbers and WHO-GMP certificates (issuing authority, number, site, validity).
4. **Your licences:** drug wholesale/sale licence, export authorisations and NOCs, the CDSCO licence quoted on the
   site, and the Pharmexcil RCMC.
5. **ISO 9001:2015:** certificate number, certification body, certified entity/site, scope, validity. (The site says
   "since 2011/2012", but the 2015 edition was published in September 2015. This must be corrected.)
6. **Registrations:** which products are registered in which countries (NAFDAC, FDA Ghana, PPB, TMDA, SAHPRA,
   EFDA, AIRP)? Send the numbers. "Dossier prepared" is not "registered".
7. **Countries actually supplied** in the last 24 months (shipping documents) and any customer we may name
   (with written consent).
8. **Zone IVb stability data** for the products you want to highlight, and Indian approvals for each fixed-dose
   combination (e.g. Ceftriaxone+Sulbactam, Ofloxacin+Ornidazole).
9. **Team:** are the people on the About page (CEO, CTO "IIT Mumbai", VP Sales) real? We will show or quote only
   real people with written consent.
10. **Headcount:** the total number of people in your company, needed for the animation software licence.

## C. Decisions we recommend you take (owner, in writing)
1. **Remove from the public website and from all marketing:**
   - Research-use-only peptides (BPC-157, TB-500, Retatrutide, MK-677, Melanotan…).
   - Nandrolone.
   - The "Herbal Sex Booster" and the sexual-wellness line.
   - "Unrestricted custom sourcing" wording.
   - Third-party brand names (Ventolin, Crocin, Becosules, Candid, Mucolite) unless you are an authorised distributor.

   Platforms review the website a post or ad links to, so these lines put every social and ad account at risk. India's
   DMR Act bans sexual-wellness and diabetes adverts, including adverts for export.
2. **Correct the website claims** listed in B2, B5 and B9, the placeholder "VERIFY-ME" tags, and the old indexed
   pages (steroids, nootropics, the "Pune" API list).
3. **Trademark check** for "AKUMA PHARMA" (an unrelated AKUMA PHARMA PRIVATE LIMITED exists in Assam).

What we can start right away, without the evidence: product-free educational videos (what a CoPP is, Zone IVb
explained, how to register with NAFDAC/PPB/FDA Ghana, how to verify a supplier). These name no products and make
no claims about the company.
