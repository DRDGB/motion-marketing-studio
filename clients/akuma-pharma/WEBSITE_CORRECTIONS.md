## 0. Purpose, true business model and global rules

### 0.1 Purpose
Make every public statement on the website, its PDF, feeds, structured data and AI files **true and provable**. Today the site presents a 17-year-old WHO-GMP/ISO manufacturer with 200+ staff, its own plants and labs, and a 224-SKU in-stock catalogue that includes peptides (added in error, the owner confirms), an anabolic steroid and erectile-dysfunction (ED) drugs. **None of that is the business.** In addition, the site displays **fabricated certificates** and hosts **~1,000 auto-generated articles, some naming another company**. These are legal risks for the owners and must go first.

### 0.2 The TRUE business model (use this, and only this)
1. **Akuma Enterprises** is a **partnership firm** with its registered office at Cabin 101, #2609, Vinod Accounting Solutions, Landran Rd, Adarsh Nagar, Kharar, SAS Nagar (Mohali), Punjab 140301, India. GSTIN 03ACLFA6940J1ZB (GST since 7 Nov 2025). PAN/IEC ACLFA6940J (IEC issued 19 Nov 2025).
2. It is a **new pharmaceutical trading and sourcing company**. It **does not manufacture anything** and has no plant, cleanroom, laboratory or QC/QA release function of its own.
3. It **sources finished medicines from WHO-GMP certified contract manufacturers in India** (its partner manufacturers) and supplies **licensed importers, distributors and institutional buyers**, mainly in Africa.
4. **WHO-GMP certificates, manufacturing licences, CoPP/FSC, stability data, dossiers, CoAs and SDSs belong to the partner manufacturers and are issued in their names.** Akuma requests, checks and forwards them, and coordinates logistics (FOB/CIF etc. from Indian ports and airports).
5. **Unknown until the client confirms**: partner names and number, countries actually supplied, team, staff count, any ISO certificate of Akuma, product registrations in African countries, real response times. These appear as `[CLIENT TO CONFIRM: …]` placeholders (full list in §9).
