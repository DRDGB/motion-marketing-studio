**Market context.** CI had 8.40M social media identities in Oct 2025. Facebook ad reach is 8.40M and Instagram only
895k (D-035). In Africascope 2025, WhatsApp leads in both CI and SN. TikTok reaches 82% of the sample in SN and 61% in
CI. Facebook holds at 80% in CI but drops to 54% in SN (D-036). Cameroon has 5.90M social identities (D-037).
→ The order to work in is TikTok + Facebook Reels + WhatsApp Status. Instagram and LinkedIn come second (INFERENCE).

**Trade dress to avoid copying.** Educarriere's "TutoCarrière" episode naming (colours not checked in this run). JAPP's video-CV
selfie framing. Canva/LinkedIn interface look. Real employer logos (SODECI, CIE, BOAD…) in any job-offer visual.

**What works (INFERENCE from the table):**
1. **Animated POV scenarios** about CV lies and interview panic are the strongest format. One proven example is from an Abidjan animator, and shares run very high (6–20% of likes).
2. A **"free / gratuit"** offer combined with a concrete deliverable ("analyse du CV", "modèle").
3. **Second-person pain hooks** in the first second: "Tu postules partout mais…", "Pas de CV ?".
4. **Before/after split screens** for ATS fixes.
5. **Local markers** such as 225/221/237 hashtags, Abidjan places and Nouchi phrases. Use them lightly and keep them consistent with the brand voice.
6. Length: humour 40–65 s, tips 25–45 s, ad-style 10–20 s.

Note: viral animations often reuse a comedian's audio. We write and voice **original** scripts only.

## 3. White space for ChapChapCV animated scenario videos
Scoring is demand × fit × ease, each 1–5. Format for all: illustrated African characters, phones, CV documents,
and a job-portal UI that is **generic and labelled "Exemple illustratif"**.

| Rank | Opportunity | D×F×E | Why (evidence) | Example French hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Ton CV, sur ton téléphone, maintenant"**: the seeker who pays a cyber café or a TikTok seller and waits, versus building it themselves on the phone | 5×5×5 = **125** | "Pas de CV ?" hook got 2.8M plays (D-011). Informal sellers charge 1,500 FCFA and post daily (D-012). The "gratuit" CV-check hook got 3.0M (D-024) | « Tu payes encore quelqu'un pour taper ton CV au cyber ? Regarde ça. » |
| 2 | **"Vraie offre ou arnaque ?"**: an animated character spots the red flags (fees, WhatsApp-only contact, "urgent", no company). Then a labelled, verified-offer flow | 5×5×4 = **100** | Official fake-recruitment warnings in 2025–26 (D-034). Scam-like job relays get high reach (D-033). Boards already say "never pay" (D-004, D-010) | « "Envoie 10 000 F pour valider ta candidature." Stop. Un vrai recruteur ne te demande jamais ça. » |
| 3 | **POV humour in local settings**: CV lies, interview panic, "relance" anxiety, with the ChapChapCV fix as the punchline | 5×4×4 = **80** | 4.3M and 1.17M plays for animated interview POVs (D-027, D-028). Local CV skits 1.9–2.2M (D-030) | « POV : t'as écrit "maîtrise parfaite d'Excel" sur ton CV… et le recruteur ouvre Excel. » |
| 4 | **"Le robot lit ton CV avant le recruteur"**: the ATS score explained visually (columns, icons, photos, keywords) | 4×5×4 = **80** | ATS explainers at 640k+ plays (D-031). Anti-Canva CV video 456k (D-031) | « Ton CV a peut-être été rejeté avant qu'un humain le lise. Voici pourquoi. » |
| 5 | **"Arrête de postuler partout"**: the match score helps you pick the offers you fit (score shown as indicative) | 4×5×4 = **80** | "Pas besoin d'envoyer une centaine de candidatures" 2.8M (D-025). Educarriere's "Vous postulez partout… aucune réponse ?" theme (D-002) | « 50 candidatures, 0 réponse ? Le problème, c'est peut-être OÙ tu postules. » |
| 6 | **"Gratuit pour créer. Tu payes seulement ton PDF, en Mobile Money"**: no card and no hidden subscription (do not name competitors) | 3×5×5 = **75** | Global builders use card-only trials that auto-renew in USD/EUR (D-018…D-021). Locals pay per document (D-012) | « Pas de carte bancaire. Pas d'abonnement caché. Tu crées gratuitement, tu payes ton PDF en Mobile Money. » |

Watch-list: **"Ton CV devient ton site"** (portfolio): 2×5×4 = 40. Proof of demand is thin. Test it once the series exists.

**Guardrails for every white space.** Never promise a job, an interview or a salary. On-screen offers, CVs and scores
carry the label "Exemple illustratif". No real employers or competitors are named or shown. Prices, the
"offre vérifiée" definition and the Mobile Money operators must be confirmed by the client in writing before
scripting. Details are in `E_PLATFORMS.md`.
