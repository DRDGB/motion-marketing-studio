# Research synthesis: ChapChapCV
_2026-11-11 · Phase 2 · tracks A (product), B (brand), C (audience), D (market), E (platforms) · 197 claims in RESEARCH_LEDGER.csv_

## The client in one line
An AI career platform from Abidjan for francophone Africa: **CV written by AI** (4 questions, import, adapt to a job ad,
ATS score, cover letter, FR/EN), **verified job offers with a match score**, and **the CV turned into a personal website**
(portfolio at chapchapcv.com/p/nom, QR code). Free to create; pay per PDF export with credits in FCFA by Mobile Money,
Wave or card (A_PRODUCT).

## The 3 strongest insights
1. **The pain is personal and urgent.** Students and young graduates (the core) have no experience to show, send
   dozens of applications without answers, pay a cyber café or a TikTok seller ~1,500–2,000 FCFA to type a CV, and fear
   fake offers asking for "frais de dossier" (C_AUDIENCE, D-012, D-033/034).
2. **Their social presence is almost empty, the format gap is wide.** LinkedIn 446 followers is the only active channel;
   Instagram 10, TikTok 44 (A_PRODUCT §6). Local job boards post tips, not stories; nobody does **animated African
   scenarios** for careers. Animated POV sketches and "Pas de CV ?" hooks reach millions in French (D-011, D-027/028).
3. **TikTok first, then Facebook Reels, WhatsApp Status, YouTube, LinkedIn**; short (25–65 s), captioned, light, French
   with a light local touch (Nouchi / Wolof / Camfranglais checked by natives), "tu", no hype (C_AUDIENCE, E_PLATFORMS).

scene (support emerald #059669) · Inter (OFL) · logo captured as PNG (no redraw) · "Chap chap" = vite (Nouchi).
Rules: white text on orange only big and bold; no orange text on green.

comment faire un CV · modèle de CV gratuit · CV sans expérience · CV pour stage · lettre de motivation stage ·
demande d'emploi · CV ATS c'est quoi · offre d'emploi Abidjan · préparer un entretien · CV canadien.

## Guardrails (E_PLATFORMS)
No promised job, interview or salary · scores shown as indicative ("indicatif") · on-screen CVs, offers and scores
labelled "Exemple illustratif" · no real employer or competitor logos · website testimonials and site statistics
not used · prices only as the site states them · drawn characters labelled "Histoire illustrative".

## Cultural calendar (C_AUDIENCE)
January (new year, new CV) · concours registration (~mid-March–April) · job fair FNER (~May) · BAC results (early July) ·
World Youth Skills Day (15 Jul) · university rentrée (mid-September) · Ramadan / Tabaski (provisional dates).
