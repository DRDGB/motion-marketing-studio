# ChapChapCV · « Chap chap, ta carrière avance » : campaign vision and first 5 videos
_2026-11-11 · Phase 3 · **Status: APPROVED 2026-11-11 (D-006)** · French first (D-003) · scenario format (D-005) · sources: 02_RESEARCH/SYNTHESIS.md_

## 1. Big idea
**« Ton CV. Ton job. Chap chap. »** Every video is a short story of a young African job seeker stuck on a real problem
(no experience, no answers, paying the cyber café, a fake offer), who finds ChapChapCV on their phone and moves forward,
fast. "Chap chap" (vite, in Nouchi) is the promise: what used to take days and money now happens in minutes, on the phone.

**Creative platform: « Le Fil Orange ».** One orange line (the brand's action colour) runs from the character's phone to
their new CV, to the job offer, to the interview: the same device that linked the story to the solution in our earlier
work, re-skinned in ChapChapCV's colours. Two worlds: **white** (daily life, the problem) and **deep emerald** (the switch:
ChapChapCV at work).

## 2. Goal and KPIs (hypotheses)
Primary: **sign-ups / CVs started** from the videos (UTM per video on `chapchapcv.com/fr/resume-builder/onboarding`).
Supporting: follows on TikTok/Instagram (starting from ~50), shares, comments, WhatsApp community joins.
Day 30: 1,000+ TikTok/IG followers, 300+ tracked visits. Day 60: 3,000+ followers, 1,000+ visits.

## 3. Voice and tone
French, **"tu"** (the brand's own social voice), short sentences, warm, a little humour, local touches (Abidjan first:
"chap chap", "go", "on est ensemble"), checked by a native speaker. No emoji in the video itself; emoji in captions.
Narrator: a young West African francophone voice (ElevenLabs library voice, commercial plan).

## 6. Cadence and platforms
3 videos/week to start: TikTok + Instagram Reels + Facebook Reels (same 9:16 file), WhatsApp Status, YouTube Shorts;
LinkedIn for CCV-002/003. Posting hypothesis: 12:30 and 19:00 GMT (Abidjan, Dakar), 19:00 WAT (Douala).
Key dates: January "nouvelle année, nouveau CV", concours season (March–April), FNER (May), BAC results (July), rentrée (Sept).

## 7. Guardrails (from E_PLATFORMS)
No promised job, interview or salary · scores "indicatifs" · CVs, offers, scores on screen labelled "Exemple illustratif" ·
characters labelled "Histoire illustrative" · no real employer/competitor logos · no website testimonials or statistics ·
prices only as written on the site (« Créez gratuitement. Payez uniquement ce que vous utilisez. »).
