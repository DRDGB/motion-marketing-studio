## What to collect per business (whatever exists)
- **Logo** (screenshot of the profile picture is enough to start; best quality you can find).
- **3–5 product or place photos** from their own posts, the ones that best show what they sell
  (the signature wig, the volcan guacamole, the wedding cake, the jar…). Note the post date.
- **Exact names** of their best-selling products / services, as they write them.
- **Their colours and fonts** if their posts have a recognisable style (or a screenshot of a typical post).
- **Anything personal**: the founder's name and how they present themselves, a slogan they repeat, a recent event.

## How we then build each video
1. I use your answers + assets to rewrite that prospect's video brief (products first, their logo on screen).
2. You approve the brief (one short screen per prospect).
3. I build the video, you watch it, we adjust, and only then is it ready to send.
