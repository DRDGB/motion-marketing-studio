## Audit (10 Nov 2026)
Posts regularly (~3/week in October) with well-written captions and clean product photos: 11 of the 12
latest posts are **static images**. Their one video (the founder interview) reached 47.5K plays, about 100× a
normal post. Someone manages the page (consistent tone), but there is **no motion at all**.
- **Read:** a real brand with a CM but no video production. The strongest proof you can show a prospect is
  their own numbers: "your only video did 47K". Pitch motion as a *complement* to their CM, not a replacement.

## Angle
**"La nature ivoirienne, ingrédient par ingrédient."** The ingredients orbit a jar of Chantilly de karité and
fall into it one at a time.

## Gift video brief (24–28 s)
Palette from their site: terracotta **#D57D55** (theme colour) + cream + deep green; soft, round shapes.
1. Hook (0–3 s): "Votre peau mérite…" / "**l'essentiel.**"
2. Orbit (3–12 s): illustrated leaves/nuts circle (Karité · Baobab · Coco · Hibiscus · Touloucouna); each name
   lands as it passes the front.
3. Into the jar (12–17 s): ingredients drop into the jar, which closes with the AYES name on it. "Chantilly de karité."
4. Made here (17–21 s): "Fabriqué en Côte d'Ivoire 🇨🇮". The map outline draws itself.
5. CTA (21–25 s): "Boutique · Livraison" + WhatsApp **07 49 25 04 39**.
6. End card: **AYES ESSENTIELLE**, "Pour une peau belle & saine, naturellement."
Compliance (cosmetics): no "certified", "heals", "anti-", "clarifying/lightening" claims. Ingredient names only
and their own tagline.
